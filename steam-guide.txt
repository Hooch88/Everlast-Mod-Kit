Everlast Mod Kit: Remove Curse, Stack Items, Free Estate Build, Craft Many 200, Change World Settings


============================================================
GUIDE DESCRIPTION
============================================================
A free tool that runs as a single web page or automated python patcher. Build your estate for free with unlimited estate points, craft up to 200 items in one batch, remove curse sources, raise stack sizes, speed up crafting, strengthen ichor/totems, boost progression and alter world rates and difficulty. Nothing to install, and you can undo your changes anytime.

What It Does


============================================================
SECTION 1 TEXT
============================================================
Everlast Mod Kit lets you change how Everlast plays without installing anything. It's a single web page that you download and open in your browser. It runs on your PC, and your files are never uploaded.

[list]

SECTION 3 TITLE
============================================================
Applying Changes via the Web Tool


============================================================
SECTION 3 TEXT
============================================================
Download and open [b]Everlast Mod Kit.html[/b] in your browser. The tool features three dedicated tabs:

[h1]Tab 1: Game Executables (No Uploads Needed!)[/h1]
If you want to customize [b]100 Estate Points[/b], [b]Craft Many 200[/b], or [b]Bank Box Access[/b]:
[olist]
[*]Open the [b]Game Executables[/b] tab at the top.
[*]Click [b]Copy 1-Click PowerShell Command[/b], press [b]Win + X[/b] -> open [b]Terminal[/b] (or [b]PowerShell[/b]), paste ([b]Ctrl + V[/b]), and hit [b]Enter[/b].
[*]Or click [b]Download PATCH_EVERLAST.bat[/b] and double-click the file!
[*]It automatically detects your Steam folder, creates safe [b].original_bak[/b] backups, and applies the changes in under a second.
[/olist]

[h1]Tab 2: The Contract (Databases & Rules)[/h1]
To change curse rules, stack sizes, prices, or respawns:
[olist]
[*]Close Everlast.
[*]Drag or choose your two database files:
[list]
[*]Assets\Database\Definitions\[b]ItemDefs.db[/b]
[*]Everlast_Data\StreamingAssets\Server\Windows\Raptor.Data\[b]Raptor.Game.db[/b]
[/list]
[*]Tick the terms you want to strike out (e.g. No Gathering Curse, 500x Stacks, Fair Peddlers).
[*]Click [b]Sign and seal[/b], then [b]Save to my game folder[/b] (or download modded files).
[/olist]

[h1]Tab 3: World Rider (Difficulty & Rates)[/h1]
To adjust XP, gathering, loot multipliers, or keep inventory on death:
[olist]
[*]Open the [b]World Rider[/b] tab and add your world's [b]worldsettings.json[/b].
[*]Customize your settings and click [b]Seal this world[/b].
[/olist]

SECTION 5 TITLE
============================================================
World Settings


============================================================
SECTION 5 TEXT
============================================================
The game locks these settings once a world is created, but your local server reads them from a file you can change.

[olist]
[*][b]Close Everlast[/b], including its local server.
[*]Open the [b]World Rider[/b] tab on the page.
[*]Drag in your world's file:
%LOCALAPPDATA%\InSpades\Everlast\Worlds\YourWorldName\[b]worldsettings.json[/b] (or Documents\My Games\Everlast\Worlds\... on older patches)
[*]Change what you want. The page shows your world's current settings, and only saves the ones you change.
[*]Click [b]Seal this world[/b].
[/olist]

Your new settings apply the next time you enter the world.

SECTION 6 TITLE

Undoing Changes


============================================================
SECTION 6 TEXT
============================================================
[b]To restore vanilla via backups:[/b]
Backups are automatically saved as [b].original_bak[/b] alongside patched files and inside the [b]ModBackups[/b] folder. Simply restore them.

[b]To restore via Steam:[/b]
In Steam, right-click Everlast and choose [b]Properties[/b], then [b]Installed Files[/b], then [b]Verify integrity of game files[/b]. Steam will restore the original files.
