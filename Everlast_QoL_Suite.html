Receipt

Both files passed their health check. Your originals have not been touched.

ChangeItem fileServer file

  1. Put the new ItemDefs.db in Everlast\Assets\Database\Definitions\, replacing the old one.
  2. Put the new Raptor.Game.db in Everlast_Data\StreamingAssets\Server\Windows\Raptor.Data\, replacing the old one.
  3. Back up your Worlds folder, then try the changes on cheap items first.
  4. To undo, rename your .original_bak files back to their original names and swap them in. Verifying the game files in Steam should also restore them.
  5. If the game updates, it may replace these files. Come back and sign again.
