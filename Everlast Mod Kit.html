Attachments

Receipt

Your changes are ready, but nothing is saved yet. They only exist in this page until you save them below. Your game folder hasn't changed.

ChangeItem fileServer file

  1. Optional, but a good safety net. Skip this if you already have backups.

  2. This replaces the two files in your game folder. Close Everlast first. Your browser will ask for permission.

  3. This saves your changes as two new files. Your browser may ask permission to download both.

  4. Select your Everlast game folder to apply or restore the binary patches on GameAssembly.dll, Raptor.Game.exe, and global-metadata.dat directly from your browser.

Your modded files are in your downloads. The game won't use them until you move them into your game folder:

  1. Put the new ItemDefs.db in SteamLibrary\steamapps\common\Everlast\Assets\Database\Definitions\, replacing the old one.
  2. Put the new Raptor.Game.db in SteamLibrary\steamapps\common\Everlast\Everlast_Data\StreamingAssets\Server\Windows\Raptor.Data\, replacing the old one.
  3. Back up your Worlds folder, then try the changes on cheap items first.
  4. To undo a change, add your files here again, untick the term and sign. Verifying the game files in Steam also restores everything.
  5. If the game updates, it may replace these files. Come back and sign again.
